# 💰 Smart Money Decision Assistant

> **See beyond the price. Decide with confidence.**

A Python-based financial decision-support application designed to help users make smarter everyday spending decisions through five practical tools.

## ✨ Features

### 💳 1. Subscription Leak Detector
- Add multiple subscriptions and monthly costs
- Calculate monthly and yearly subscription spending
- Identify the most expensive subscription
- Check potential yearly savings from cancellation

### 📊 2. Where Is My Money Going?
- Track spending across different categories
- Calculate total monthly spending
- Identify the highest-spending category
- Understand the percentage share of each category

### 🧾 3. Hidden Cost Calculator
- Enter the advertised product price
- Add accessories, warranty, maintenance, and other fees
- Calculate the estimated actual cost
- See how much additional costs increase the purchase price

### 💰 4. Can I Afford This?
- Enter income, expenses, savings, and purchase price
- Calculate remaining money after expenses
- Compare the purchase with available money
- Understand how much savings the purchase may use

### 💸 5. EMI Reality Checker
- Enter product price and down payment
- Enter interest rate and repayment period
- Calculate loan amount
- Calculate monthly EMI
- Calculate total interest and total repayment
- Handles zero-interest EMI calculations

## 🛠️ Technologies

- **Python**
- **Tkinter**
- **Visual Studio Code**

## ⚙️ How It Works

The application starts with a main dashboard containing the five financial tools.

1. Select a financial tool
2. Enter the required information
3. Validate the input
4. Perform the required calculations
5. Display the result and financial insight
